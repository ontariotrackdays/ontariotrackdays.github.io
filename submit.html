Know a track day we're missing, an organizer who should be listed, or a motorsport garage or shop for the directory? Send it in — it takes a couple of minutes.

How this works: every submission is reviewed before anything is published, and event details are checked against the organizer's own published information — so please include a link to where the details appear (an event page, a MotorsportReg listing, or a Facebook Event). Listings are free.

When you submit, a copy of what you sent is emailed to you, with a copy to ontariotrackdays@gmail.com — so nothing gets lost. Your name and email are never published.
