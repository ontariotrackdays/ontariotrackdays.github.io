The tracks

Toronto Motorsports Park — Cayuga

House open car lapping (cash at the gate), Pinnacle/PADA, Touge.ca, special events like Bimmerfest Canada, and winter lapping.

Grand Bend Motorplex

Organizer and club lapping days, Euro Klassik weekend lapping. A track in transition for 2027 — watch this space.

Canadian Tire Motorsport Park — DDT

Driver development track: Touge.ca, Motori, OTA school, club days. Configurations vary by event — each listing names the one in use.

Canadian Tire Motorsport Park — GP

The big track: DriveTeq, Trillium BMW, PCA UCR, OTA, Infinite Motorsports. Mostly intermediate/advanced.

Shannonville Motorsport Park

Motion Monday evenings, GT Race days, St. LAC evenings, Touge.ca. Four configurations — the listing always names the layout.

Calabogie Motorsports Park

Canada's longest track. Most lapping requires a prior sign-off to run solo; Track Nights and HPDE days run spring to fall.

About us

Ontario Track Days+ is a free community calendar of car track days across Ontario — HPDE, open lapping, driver schools, Time Attack from Ontario Time Attack and CSCS, special events that include public open lapping, and winter lapping. It's curated by Paul Murray and modelled on Ontario Vendor Events: one place to check dates, times, costs and registration links instead of chasing Facebook groups and organizer sites.

Listings are taken from organizer-published sources only, and each one shows when it was last verified. Nothing here is paywalled and there are no accounts — your saved events and settings stay in your own browser.

Tracks covered: Toronto Motorsports Park (Cayuga), Grand Bend Motorplex, Canadian Tire Motorsport Park (DDT and GP), Shannonville Motorsport Park, and Calabogie Motorsports Park. Cars only — no motorcycle days, no autocross, no CASC test days.

Alerts

How web notifications work here: this page checks for new matches and due reminders when you open it, and shows a browser notification then if you've enabled them. A static web page can't run in the background — for alerts that arrive on their own (a morning digest of new dates, instant cancellation notices), the Android app is the way, and it's in the plan. Meanwhile, subscribing to the calendar feed lets your own calendar app remind you about any event you add from it.
